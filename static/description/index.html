Supplier Performance & Risk Scorecard

Measure supplier performance and identify purchasing risks

Evaluate supplier delivery performance, monitor receipt completeness, identify potential risks, and track corrective actions through a centralized supplier evaluation system.

Key Features

Supplier Evaluation

  • Supplier scorecards by supplier, company, and period
  • Delivery timeliness indicators
  • Purchase receipt completeness indicators
  • Weighted supplier performance scores

Risk Management

  • Supplier risk levels and data confidence indicators
  • Historical evaluations and calculation notes
  • Batch scorecard generation
  • Risk alerts and follow-up activities
  • Resolution workflow for supplier risks

Supplier Performance Dashboard

Monitor supplier performance using company-level KPIs and visual indicators.

Business Benefits

  • Identify suppliers requiring closer monitoring
  • Compare supplier performance over evaluation periods
  • Support purchasing decisions with measurable indicators
  • Maintain a traceable history of supplier assessments

Compatibility

Odoo 19 Community Edition

Required modules: purchase_stock and mail, in addition to their dependencies.

French translations are included.

Calculation Notes

Delivery timeliness is calculated using the scheduled dates currently recorded on completed receipts. Receipt completeness is based on quantities currently received.

Each saved evaluation preserves its score and calculation notes, providing a historical record of the assessment.
